# Held plates

Held on 2026-10-11 by the curator-in-chief (Claude) after the records verifier raised them.

- **G-008003 · Voyager Golden Record cover** and **G-008004 · Pioneer plaque**. The photographs are NASA works,
  but the engraved designs they show are by Carl Sagan, Frank Drake and Linda Salzman Sagan, who were not federal
  employees. In the United States the designs are public domain (published 1972 and 1977 without a copyright notice),
  but jurisdictions without that formality, such as Australia and the EU, protect them for life + 70 years.
  The rights rule says: when you are not sure, do not shelve it.

To reshelve: a statement from the rights holders, or a clear ruling that the designs are free outside the US.
The NASA text describing each object stays on the shelves (it is a US federal work).
