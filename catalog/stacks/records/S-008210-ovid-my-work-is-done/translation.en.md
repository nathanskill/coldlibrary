And now I have finished a work that neither Jupiter's anger nor fire
nor iron nor gnawing age will be able to destroy.
Let that day, which has no claim on anything but this body,
end when it will the span of my uncertain life:
yet in my better part I shall be carried, enduring,
above the high stars, and my name will never be erased;
wherever Roman power reaches over the lands it has mastered,
I shall be read on the lips of the people, and through all the ages, in fame,
if the forecasts of poets hold any truth, I shall live.
