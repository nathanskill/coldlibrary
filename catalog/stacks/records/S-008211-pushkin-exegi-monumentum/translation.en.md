Exegi monumentum

I have raised myself a monument not made by hands;
the people's path to it will not be overgrown;
it has lifted its unbowed head higher
  than the Alexandrian Column.

No, I shall not wholly die: my soul in the treasured lyre
will outlive my dust and escape decay,
and I shall be renowned as long as in the world beneath the moon
  a single poet is alive.

Word of me will travel through all great Rus',
and every tongue that is in her will call my name:
the proud grandson of the Slavs, and the Finn, and the still untamed
  Tungus, and the Kalmyk, friend of the steppes.

And long shall I be dear to the people, because
I woke kind feelings with my lyre,
because in my cruel age I sang of Freedom
  and called for mercy to the fallen.

Be obedient, O Muse, to the will of God;
fearing no insult, asking for no crown,
accept praise and slander alike with indifference,
  and do not argue with a fool.

21 August 1836
