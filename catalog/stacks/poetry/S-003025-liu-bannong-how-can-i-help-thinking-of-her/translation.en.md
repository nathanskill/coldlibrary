A few light clouds are floating in the sky,
a light breeze is blowing over the earth.
Ah!
The breeze stirs my hair;
how can I help thinking of her?

The moonlight is in love with the sea,
the sea is in love with the moonlight.
Ah!
On a silver night as sweet as honey,
how can I help thinking of her?

On the water, fallen petals slowly drift,
under the water, little fish slowly swim.
Ah!
Swallow, what are you saying?
How can I help thinking of her?

Withered trees sway in the cold wind,
a wild fire burns in the dusk.
Ah!
There is still a little afterglow in the western sky;
how can I help thinking of her?

(4 September 1920, London)
