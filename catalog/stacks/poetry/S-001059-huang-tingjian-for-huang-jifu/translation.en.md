I live by the northern sea, you by the southern;
I would send a letter by the wild goose, but it declines.
Peach and plum in the spring wind, one cup of wine;
rivers and lakes in the night rain, ten years of lamplight.
To keep house you have only four bare walls;
to heal the sick you need no thrice-broken arm.
I imagine you reading, your hair already white,
across the stream, apes crying in the vines of the fever mist.
