Among the flowers, a jug of wine;
I drink alone, with no one close.
I raise my cup to invite the bright moon,
and with my shadow we make three.
The moon does not know how to drink,
my shadow only follows me about;
still, for now, with moon and shadow for company,
I will take my pleasure while it is spring.
I sing, and the moon lingers;
I dance, and my shadow sways and scatters.
While sober we share our joy;
once drunk we go our separate ways.
Let us be friends for ever, beyond all feeling,
and meet again far off in the River of Heaven.
