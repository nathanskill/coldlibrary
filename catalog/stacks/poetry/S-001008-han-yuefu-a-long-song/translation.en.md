Green, green, the mallows in the garden,
the morning dew waiting for the sun to dry it.
Bright spring spreads its kindness,
and all things come out shining.
I always fear the autumn coming,
when the flowers and leaves turn brown and fall.
A hundred rivers flow east to the sea:
when will any of them come back west?
If you do not work hard while young and strong,
you will only grieve when you are old.
