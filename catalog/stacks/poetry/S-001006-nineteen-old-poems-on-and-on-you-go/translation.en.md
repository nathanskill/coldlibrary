On and on, and on you go,
parted from me while we both still live.
Ten thousand li now lie between us,
each at one end of the sky.
The road is hard and long:
who knows when we will meet?
The northern horse leans into the north wind,
the southern bird nests on the southern bough.
Each day the distance grows,
each day my sash hangs looser.
Drifting clouds cover the white sun;
the traveller does not think of coming back.
Missing you is making me old,
and the year has suddenly grown late.
Let it go, and say no more.
Be well: eat, and keep your strength.
