Far away, the Herdboy star;
bright, bright, the Weaving Girl of the River of Heaven.
Slender, slender, she lifts her white hands,
click, clack, working the loom.
All day she cannot finish a single pattern,
her tears falling like rain.
The River of Heaven is clear and shallow:
how far apart can they be?
Brimming, one stretch of water between them;
gazing, gazing, and no word spoken.
