Fine clouds work their clever patterns,
shooting stars carry their sorrow,
far across the silver river, in secret, they cross.
One meeting of golden wind and jade dew
is better than countless meetings in the human world.

Tender feeling like water,
the happy hour like a dream:
how can they bear to look back at the magpie-bridge road home?
If love between two is to last,
why must it be every morning and every evening?
