Yu, yu, the deer are calling,
grazing on the wild sedge.
I have good guests with me:
play the zither, blow the reed-pipes.
Blow the pipes and sound the reeds,
bring out the baskets full of gifts.
These friends who care for me
will show me the right road.

Yu, yu, the deer are calling,
grazing on the wild wormwood.
I have good guests with me,
their good name shining clear.
They show the people how not to be mean;
the gentlemen take them as their pattern.
I have fine wine:
let my guests feast and be at ease.

Yu, yu, the deer are calling,
grazing on the wild herbs.
I have good guests with me:
pluck the zithers, great and small.
Pluck the zithers, great and small,
in harmony and lasting joy.
I have fine wine
to gladden the hearts of my guests.
