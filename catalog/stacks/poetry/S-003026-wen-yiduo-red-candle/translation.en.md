"Only when the candle turns to ash are its tears dry." — Li Shangyin

Red candle!
So red a candle!
Poet!
Bring out your heart and compare them:
are they the same colour?

Red candle!
Who made the wax to give you a body?
Who lit the flame to kindle your soul?
Why must the wax burn down to ash
before you can give out light?
Error upon error;
contradiction, conflict!

Red candle!
No error, no error!
it is by "burning" that your light must come:
this is the way of nature.

Red candle!
Since you are made, then burn!
Burn on, burn on!
Burn through the dreams of the world,
set the blood of the world boiling,
save their souls,
and break open their prisons!

Red candle!
The hour your heart's fire gives out light
is the day your tears begin to flow.

Red candle!
The craftsman who made you
made you to burn.
Now that you are burning,
why grieve and weep?
Oh, now I know!
It is the stray wind that comes to harry your light;
when your flame burns unsteadily,
you weep in your distress.

Red candle!
Flow on! How can you help but flow?
Let your tallow
run without rest into the world,
to grow flowers of comfort
and bear the fruit of joy.

Red candle!
For every tear you shed, your heart turns a little more to ash.
A heart of ash and tears: that is your fruit;
creating light: that is your seed.

Red candle!
"Ask not of the harvest; ask only of the tilling."
