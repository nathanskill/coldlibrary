If I were a snowflake
drifting freely in mid-air,
I would know my direction:
flying, flying, flying,
here on this earth is my direction.

Not to the cold, lonely valley,
not to the bleak foot of the hills,
nor down the empty street to brood:
flying, flying, flying,
you see, I have my direction.

Dancing gracefully in mid-air,
I would find that quiet, clear dwelling
and wait for her to come into the garden:
flying, flying, flying,
ah, the scent of red plum blossom about her.

Then, light as I am,
I would softly settle on the folds of her dress,
close to her heart, gentle as a wave:
melting, melting, melting,
melting into her heart, gentle as a wave.
