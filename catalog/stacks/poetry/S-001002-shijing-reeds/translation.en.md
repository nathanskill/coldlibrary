The reeds are deep and grey-green,
the white dew has turned to frost.
The one I am thinking of
is on the far side of the water.
I go upstream to follow: the way is hard and long.
I go downstream to follow: there, as if in the middle of the stream.

The reeds are thick and lush,
the white dew not yet dry.
The one I am thinking of
is at the water's edge.
I go upstream to follow: the way is hard and steep.
I go downstream to follow: there, as if on an islet in the stream.

The reeds are bright and full,
the white dew not yet gone.
The one I am thinking of
is on the water's bank.
I go upstream to follow: the way is hard and winding.
I go downstream to follow: there, as if on a sandbar in the stream.
