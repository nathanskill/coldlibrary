These years the taste of the world has grown thin as gauze;
who made me ride a horse to be a guest in the capital?
All night in the little upstairs room I listened to the spring rain;
tomorrow morning, down the deep lanes, they will be selling apricot blossom.
On short paper, in slanting lines, I idly write cursive;
by a sunny window, with fine froth, I play at making tea.
White robe, do not sigh about the dust of the road:
there is still time to be home by Qingming.
