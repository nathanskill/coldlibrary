I built my hut among the homes of men
and hear no noise of carriages and horses.
You ask how this can be?
When the heart is far, the place grows remote of itself.
I pick chrysanthemums by the eastern hedge
and, at ease, see the southern hill.
The mountain air is fine at dusk,
the birds fly home together.
In this there is a true meaning;
I want to explain it, and have already forgotten the words.
