In this life we seldom meet;
we move like the Shen and Shang stars.
And tonight, what night is this,
that we share the light of this lamp?
How long do youth and strength last?
Our hair at the temples is already grey.
I ask after old friends: half are ghosts;
I cry out in surprise, my heart burning.
How could I know that after twenty years
I would come again into your hall?
When we parted you were not yet married;
now your sons and daughters stand in a row.
Cheerfully they greet their father's friend
and ask me where I have come from.
Before the questions and answers are done,
you send the children to set out the wine.
In the night rain they cut spring chives;
fresh-cooked rice is mixed with yellow millet.
You say how hard it is for us to meet,
and raise your cup ten times in a row.
Ten cups, and I am still not drunk;
I am moved by how long your old friendship lasts.
Tomorrow mountains will lie between us,
and the world's affairs will be lost in mist for both.
