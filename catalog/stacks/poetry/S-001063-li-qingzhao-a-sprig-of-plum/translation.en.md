The red lotus's scent is fading, the jade mat is cool with autumn.
Lightly I loosen my silk skirt
and step alone into the orchid boat.
Who, from among the clouds, is sending me a letter?
When the geese come back in their lines,
moonlight fills the western tower.

Flowers fall of themselves, water flows of itself.
One kind of longing,
idle sorrow in two places.
There is no way to be rid of this feeling:
it has only just left my brow
and is back again in my heart.
