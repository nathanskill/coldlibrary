What is a life, wherever it goes, most like?
A wild goose stepping on snowy mud.
On the mud, by chance, it leaves the print of its claws;
when the goose flies off, does it care whether east or west?
The old monk has died and become a new stupa;
on the broken wall there is no way to see what we once wrote.
Do you still remember how hard the road was then?
The way was long, we were tired, and the lame donkey brayed.
