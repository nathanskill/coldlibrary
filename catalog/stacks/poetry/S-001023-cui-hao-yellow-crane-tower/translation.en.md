The man of old has ridden off on the yellow crane;
nothing is left here but Yellow Crane Tower.
The yellow crane, once gone, never came back;
for a thousand years the white clouds have drifted, empty.
Across the clear river, the Hanyang trees stand out one by one;
the fragrant grass grows thick on Parrot Island.
The day is ending: where is my home?
Mist on the river waves makes a man sad.
