For once this old man lets loose a young man's wildness:
a yellow hound on my left, a grey falcon on my right.
In brocade caps and sable furs,
a thousand riders sweep across the level ridge.
Tell the whole city to follow its prefect:
I will shoot the tiger myself, like young Sun Quan.

Warm with wine, my heart and nerve are bold;
a little frost at my temples: what of it?
When will they send an envoy with the tally to Yunzhong,
as they once sent Feng Tang?
I will draw the carved bow round as the full moon,
look to the northwest, and shoot down the Wolf Star.
