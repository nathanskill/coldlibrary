As a youth I listened to rain above the singing house,
red candles dim behind the silk curtains.
In my prime I listened to rain on a traveller's boat,
the river wide, the clouds low, a lost goose crying in the west wind.

And now I listen to rain under a monk's eaves,
my temples already starred with grey.
Joy and grief, parting and meeting, have no feeling for us:
let the rain drip on the steps until the dawn.
