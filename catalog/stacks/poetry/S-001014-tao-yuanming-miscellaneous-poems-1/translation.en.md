Our lives have no roots or stems;
we drift like dust upon the road,
scattered, turning with the wind:
already we are not the selves we were.
Born into this world, we are all brothers;
why must it be only those of one blood?
When there is joy, let us be glad of it,
with a jug of wine to bring the neighbours in.
The prime of life does not come twice;
a day has only one morning.
Make good use of the time while it is here:
the years and months wait for no one.
