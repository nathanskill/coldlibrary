The walls and towers of the capital, guarded by the lands of Qin;
through wind and mist I look toward the Five Fords.
The feeling of this parting, you and I share:
we are both officials far from home.
Within the four seas, if you have one who knows you,
the ends of the sky are like next door.
So do not, where our roads divide,
wet our scarves with tears like boys and girls.
