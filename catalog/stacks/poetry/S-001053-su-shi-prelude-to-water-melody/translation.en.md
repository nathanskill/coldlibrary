Mid-Autumn of the year Bingchen. I drank happily until dawn, got very drunk, and wrote this, thinking also of Ziyou.

How long has the bright moon been there?
I raise my cup and ask the blue sky.
I do not know, in the palaces of heaven,
what year it is tonight.
I would like to ride the wind and go back up there,
but I fear those towers of jade and halls of crystal, so high, so very cold.
I rise and dance with my clear shadow:
how could that compare with being among people?

It turns past the vermilion pavilion,
it stoops through the carved window,
it shines on the sleepless.
It should bear no grudge,
so why is it always full when people are apart?
People have sorrow and joy, parting and meeting;
the moon is dim or bright, full or waning;
this has never been whole since ancient times.
I only wish that we may both live long
and share, a thousand li apart, the beauty of the moon.
