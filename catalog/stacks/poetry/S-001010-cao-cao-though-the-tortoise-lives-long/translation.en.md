The sacred tortoise lives long,
but its time comes to an end.
The flying serpent rides the mist,
and turns to dust and ash at last.
The old warhorse lies in its stall,
its heart still set on a thousand li;
a man of spirit in his later years
does not give up his great heart.
The span of a life, long or short,
is not decided by heaven alone;
the blessing of a calm and cheerful mind
can bring you many years.
What fortune, what joy:
I sing to tell my mind.
