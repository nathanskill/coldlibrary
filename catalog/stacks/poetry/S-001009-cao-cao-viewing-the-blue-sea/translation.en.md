East I came to Jieshi
to look upon the blue sea.
How the waters heave and roll,
how high the island hills stand.
Trees grow thick there,
the hundred grasses lush.
The autumn wind is sighing,
great waves surge and rise.
The journey of the sun and moon
seems to begin inside it;
the Milky Way in its splendour
seems to rise from within.
What fortune, what joy:
I sing to tell my mind.
