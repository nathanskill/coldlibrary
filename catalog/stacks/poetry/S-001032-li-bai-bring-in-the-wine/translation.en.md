Have you not seen
the Yellow River's waters coming down from heaven,
rushing to the sea, never to return?
Have you not seen
in the high hall's bright mirror, the grief of white hair,
black silk at morning, snow by evening?
When life goes your way, take all the joy there is;
don't let the golden cup stand empty to the moon.
Heaven made me, and my gifts must have their use;
spend a thousand in gold and it will all come back.
Cook a sheep, kill an ox, and let us be merry;
we ought to drink three hundred cups at one sitting.
Master Cen,
my friend Danqiu,
bring in the wine,
and do not put down your cups.
Let me sing you a song;
please lend me your ears and listen.
Bells and drums and jade-fine food are not worth prizing;
I only want to stay drunk and never wake.
The sages and worthies of old are all left in silence;
only the drinkers have kept their names.
The Prince of Chen once feasted at Pingle Lodge,
ten thousand a jar for wine, joking as he pleased.
Why, host, do you say that you are short of money?
Go straight out and buy more, and pour for us.
My dappled horse,
my thousand-gold furs:
call the boy, take them out and trade them for good wine,
and with you I will drown the sorrows of ten thousand ages.
