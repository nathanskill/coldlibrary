## I
Half an acre of square pond opens like a mirror;
the light of the sky and the shadows of clouds drift in it together.
I ask it how it can be so clear:
because fresh water keeps flowing in from the source.

## II
Last night by the river the spring flood rose;
the great warship floated, light as a feather.
All the effort spent pushing it before was wasted;
today it moves at ease in the middle of the stream.
