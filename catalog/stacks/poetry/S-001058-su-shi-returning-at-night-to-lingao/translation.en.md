Drinking at night on the Eastern Slope, sober and drunk again,
I come home at what seems the third watch.
The houseboy's breathing is already thunder.
I knock, and no one answers;
leaning on my staff, I listen to the river.

I have long regretted that this self is not my own:
when will I forget all this busy striving?
The night is late, the wind is still, the ripples are smooth.
In a little boat I will go from here,
and trust the rest of my life to the rivers and the sea.
