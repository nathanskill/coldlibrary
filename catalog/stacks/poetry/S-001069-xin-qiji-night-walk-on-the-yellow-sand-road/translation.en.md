The bright moon startles a magpie from its branch;
in the cool breeze at midnight the cicadas sing.
In the scent of the rice flowers there is talk of a good year:
listen, the frogs, one great chorus.

Seven or eight stars beyond the sky,
two or three drops of rain before the hill.
The old thatched inn beside the shrine wood:
the road turns at the stream bridge, and suddenly there it is.
