Fair tree of heaven and earth,
the orange came and made this land its home.
Bound by its nature not to move,
it grows in the southern country.
Deep-rooted, hard to transplant,
it is all the more of one mind.
Green leaves and white blossoms,
abundant and a joy to see.
Layered branches, sharp thorns,
and the round fruit full.
Green and yellow mingled,
its colours bright as a pattern.
Clear-skinned and white within,
like one fit to carry the Way.
Lush and well tended,
lovely, with nothing ugly in it.

Ah, from your youth your purpose
had something different in it.
Standing alone and not moving:
how could that not be a joy?
Deep-rooted, hard to transplant,
open-hearted and asking nothing.
Awake to the world and standing alone,
crossing the current, not drifting with it.
Guarding your heart, careful of yourself,
you never fall into error.
Holding to virtue without self-interest,
you stand level with heaven and earth.
Through all the withering of the years
let me be your lasting friend.
Fine and composed, never wanton,
upright, with grain in you.
Though young in years,
you could be a teacher to your elders.
Your conduct matches Bo Yi's:
I set you up as my pattern.
