How long has there been a moon in the blue sky?
I put down my cup for a moment to ask.
People reach for the bright moon and cannot catch it,
yet when the moon travels it keeps us company.
Bright as a flying mirror above the red palace gates,
when the green mists clear it pours out its pure light.
We only see it come up out of the sea at night;
who knows where it sinks among the clouds at dawn?
The white hare pounds its medicine, autumn after spring;
Chang'e lives alone: who are her neighbours?
People today never saw the moon of old,
but this moon once shone on the people of old.
People of old and people today are like flowing water;
all of them have looked at the bright moon like this.
I only wish that while we sing and drink
the moonlight would shine long into the golden cup.
