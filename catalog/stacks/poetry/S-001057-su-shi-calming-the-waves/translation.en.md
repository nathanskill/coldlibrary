Do not listen to the rain beating through the woods and on the leaves;
why not chant and whistle and walk on slowly?
Bamboo staff and straw sandals, lighter than a horse:
who's afraid?
A straw cape in the misty rain, and let my whole life go so.

The brisk spring wind blows me sober,
a little cold;
over the hill the slanting sun comes out to meet me.
I look back at the place where it was bleak and blowing,
and go home:
no wind, no rain, and no clear sky either.
