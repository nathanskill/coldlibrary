The sky meets the cloud-waves and the morning mist;
the River of Stars is about to turn, a thousand sails dancing.
My dreaming soul seems to return to the dwelling of heaven.
I hear heaven's voice
asking me, kindly, where I am going home to.

I answer: the road is long, and I sigh that the day grows late;
I studied poetry, and have lines that startle, for nothing.
Ninety thousand li on the wind, the great roc is rising.
Wind, do not stop:
blow my little boat away to the Three Islands.
