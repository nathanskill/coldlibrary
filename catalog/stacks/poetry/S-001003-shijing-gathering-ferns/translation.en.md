We gather ferns, we gather ferns,
the ferns are sprouting now.
Going home, going home,
the year is closing now.
No house, no home,
because of the Xianyun.
No time to sit or rest,
because of the Xianyun.

We gather ferns, we gather ferns,
the ferns are tender now.
Going home, going home,
our hearts are anxious now.
Our anxious hearts are burning,
we hunger and we thirst.
Our post is never settled,
no one is sent home with word.

We gather ferns, we gather ferns,
the ferns are tough now.
Going home, going home,
the tenth month has come now.
The king's service never ends,
no time to sit or stay.
Our anxious hearts are aching:
we set out, and have not come back.

What is that in flower?
The blossom of the bush cherry.
What is that great carriage?
The carriage of our lord.
The war chariots are harnessed,
the four stallions tall and strong.
How could we dare to settle?
In one month, three victories.

They drive the four stallions,
the four stallions strong and steady.
The lord rides in the chariot,
the foot soldiers shelter behind it.
The four stallions in good order,
ivory bow-tips, sealskin quivers.
How could we not be watchful every day?
The Xianyun press us hard.

When I set out long ago,
the willows were soft and swaying.
Now, as I come back,
the snow is falling thick.
The road is slow and long,
I thirst and I hunger.
My heart is sad and aching,
and no one knows my grief.
