The hills of Ba, the waters of Chu, a desolate country,
where for twenty-three years I lay cast aside.
Remembering old friends, I can only chant the poem about the flute;
coming home, I am like the woodcutter whose axe handle rotted.
Beside the sunken boat a thousand sails go by;
in front of the sick tree ten thousand trees are in spring.
Today, hearing you sing your song for me,
I will borrow some spirit from a cup of wine.
