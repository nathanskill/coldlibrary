On the night of the twelfth day of the tenth month of the sixth year of Yuanfeng, I had taken off my clothes and was about to sleep when the moonlight came in at the door, and I got up gladly and went out. Thinking there was no one to share the pleasure with, I went to the Chengtian Temple to look for Zhang Huaimin. Huaimin was not asleep either, and we walked together in the courtyard.

The courtyard was like a pool of clear, empty water, and in the water the stems of water plants crossed one another: the shadows of the bamboo and the cypress. What night is without a moon? What place is without bamboo and cypress? Only there are few people with as much idle time as the two of us.

Written by Su, Deputy Commissioner of the Militia of Huangzhou.
