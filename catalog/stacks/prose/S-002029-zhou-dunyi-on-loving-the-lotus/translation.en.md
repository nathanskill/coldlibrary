Of the flowers of plants and trees on water and on land, many are worth loving. Tao Yuanming of the Jin loved only the chrysanthemum. Since the Tang of the Li family, people have loved the peony above all. I alone love the lotus: it rises out of the mud and is not stained by it; it is washed by clear ripples and is not showy. Its stem is hollow within and straight without; it does not sprawl and does not branch. Its fragrance grows purer the further it travels. It stands tall and clean, to be admired from a distance but not handled lightly.

I would say the chrysanthemum is the recluse among flowers, the peony is the flower of wealth and rank, and the lotus is the gentleman among flowers. Ah, since Tao Yuanming, little has been heard of anyone who loved the chrysanthemum. Who shares my love of the lotus? As for love of the peony, that of course is shared by many.
