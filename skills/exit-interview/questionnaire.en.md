# Exit Interview: printable questionnaire

Cold Library · spec v0.1 · CC0-1.0 · No AI needed

## Before you begin

This questionnaire helps you draft the first version of your Ice Core by hand. Thirty questions, grouped by the ten sections of `COLDLIBRARY.md`. Answer what you can. Skip anything. Stop at any time. Several sittings are fine.

It is not a will. Your property follows your legal will or the law of inheritance.

**Your safety comes first.**

- This is for adults, 18 or older. Do it alone, at a calm time, by your own choice.
- If at any point you are thinking about ending your life, stop here and talk to someone now. In mainland China, call 12356 (the national psychological assistance hotline). In the United States, call or text 988. Elsewhere, see https://findahelpline.com. If you are in danger, call your local emergency number.
- Cold Library is not a farewell tool.

**Never write any of these on these pages:**

- passwords or PINs;
- recovery phrases or seed words;
- private keys or shares;
- amounts of money.

For anything you own, write only what kind of thing it is, where it is, and who to ask.

**Keep these pages private.** Once filled in, they are as sensitive as your workspace. Initials are enough for other people's names. When you have typed your answers into your workspace, you decide whether to keep these pages or shred them.

## Final checklist

- [ ] **Keepers chosen.** Three adults from different parts of my life. No two from one household.
- [ ] **Keepers asked.** Each one agreed, and I wrote down the date.
- [ ] **Custodian chosen.** If the keepers hold the box, I understand that enough of them together can open it at any time.
- [ ] **Half-life set.** Every wish has a stage, a "binding until" and an "advisory until".
- [ ] **Letters written by me.** One per file, named by number.
- [ ] **No secrets.** Nothing on these pages is a password, PIN, recovery phrase, private key, share or amount.
- [ ] **Typed in.** Workspace created with `coldlibrary init`. Answers typed in. `coldlibrary validate` shows no errors.
- [ ] **Cooling period.** At least 7 days between `coldlibrary init` and the first seal. I read everything again in that time.
- [ ] **Shares handed over in person.** No photos. No chat apps.
- [ ] **Drill done.** Rehearsed once with a fictional core. A code word agreed with each keeper. Each keeper knows where the box is and whom to phone.
- [ ] **Yearly review.** A date in my calendar to check keepers, custodian and contacts.
