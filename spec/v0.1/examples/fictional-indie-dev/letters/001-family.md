# For Sam

> Fictional example. Sam and Wren Halloway are invented people.
> This letter is sample text for the Cold Library format.

Sam,

If you are reading this, I have died and the keepers have opened the box.
I am sorry you have the paperwork. I tried to keep it short.

Start with section 4 of COLDLIBRARY.md and the grey folder in the desk. The
will is with the notary. You do not need to understand the servers. Tobiah does.

Please take the cat. She likes the windowsill in the morning and does not like
the vacuum cleaner.

You do not have to keep anything of mine out of duty. Keep what you like and
give away the rest.

Thank you for saying yes when I asked you to be a keeper.

Wren
